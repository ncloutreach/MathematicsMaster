The game is designed as an interactive journey in which a rabbit must reach its home in the forest by correctly solving arithmetic problems involving positive and negative numbers. The player begins at the starting point and helps the rabbit move forward step by step.

At each step, an arithmetic equation appears on clicking a stone. If the player solves the equation correctly, the rabbit moves forward. An incorrect answer causes the rabbit to step back and delaying its progress. Some stones are highlighted in green acting as bonus stones. If the player correctly solves the problem on a green stone, the rabbit instantly leaps to the next green stone, accelerating the journey. However, if the player fails to solve a green stone equation, the rabbit is forced to step back.

The game is time-bound, adding excitement and challenge. The player must ensure that the rabbit reaches its forest home before the timer runs out. The mix of forward and backward movements, rewards through bonus stones and the time limit makes the game both engaging and educational. 

The design ensures that children repeatedly practice solving of arithmetic problem while staying motivated through the story-like progression of helping the rabbit safely reach its home.
